Dragon Noodles - Privacy Policy

Effective & Last Updated: March 18, 2026
繁體中文 (Chinese)

Thank you for using Dragon Noodles (龍城麵線) ("the Service", "we", "us", or "our"). We take your privacy and the security of your personal information very seriously. This Privacy Policy describes how Dragon Noodles collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you access our online ordering application at https://dragon.twabc.com, including when you sign in using third-party services such as Google Sign-In, as well as the rights available to you regarding your personal data.

📌 Core Privacy Commitment: Dragon Noodles strictly utilizes the information you provide solely for order fulfillment, meal preparation, pickup notifications, and user authentication. We never sell your personal information, and we never use or transfer your data for advertising, marketing, or any unrelated purposes.

1. Information We Collect and How It Is Collected

When you visit or place orders through our online ordering platform, we collect the following necessary categories of information upon your consent:

  • Information Collected via Google Sign-In:
    • Basic Profile Information: Your Google Account display name, profile photo URL.
    • Contact Information: Your Google Account email address.
    • Google Unique Identifier (Google User ID): Used securely to link your user session and order history with your account.
  • Order & Transaction Data: Items ordered, quantities, flavor customizations, add-on selections, order amount, dining option (Takeout / Dine-in / Delivery), timestamp, and payment status.
  • Customer Contact Phone (Optional): Provided voluntarily by the customer for order status SMS alerts, store pickup phone confirmations, or courier delivery contact.
  • Technical & System Logs: Browser type, operating system, timestamp, and IP address collected automatically for server security, performance monitoring, and anti-abuse protection.

2. Purposes and Legal Basis for Data Processing

We process your personal information strictly for legitimate, specific business purposes in connection with restaurant services:

  1. User Authentication: Providing frictionless, secure authentication through Google Sign-In to protect your shopping cart and order history.
  2. Order Fulfillment & Food Preparation: Transmitting meal specifications and customer notes to our kitchen staff to prepare meals accurately and coordinate customer pickup.
  3. Real-time Status Notifications: Updating you on your order status (e.g., Received, Preparing, Ready for Pickup).
  4. Customer Support & Auditing: Handling inquiries, refund requests, and tax accounting reconciliation.
  5. System Security & Fraud Prevention: Protecting our servers against automated abuse, fraudulent orders, and Denial-of-Service attacks.

3. Google User Data Limited Use Disclosure

Dragon Noodles's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other application strictly adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:

  • We only use Google user data to provide or improve customer-facing features that are prominent in the Dragon Noodles online ordering interface.
  • We do not transfer Google user data to third parties, unless necessary to provide or improve our meal ordering service (e.g., encrypted cloud database storage on Supabase), or to comply with applicable laws.
  • We do not use or transfer Google user data for serving ads, including personalized, re-targeted, or interest-based advertising.
  • We do not allow humans to read Google user data unless we have obtained your affirmative agreement for specific customer support issues, or as required for system security investigations or legal compliance.
  • We never use your Google user data to train generalized artificial intelligence (AI) or machine learning (ML) models.

4. Data Security & Retention

  • Data Security: All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is secured using industry-standard SSL/TLS 256-bit encryption. Data is stored in secure cloud data centers certified under ISO 27001 and SOC 2, protected by database Row Level Security (RLS) policies.
  • Data Retention: Account profile information is retained until you request account deletion. Completed transaction records are retained for the statutory period required by local commercial and tax accounting laws, after which they are de-identified or securely deleted.
